From an interest to a thoughtful first email

  1. Find relevant research. Choose an interest or search the directory. A research profile helps personalize your results.
  2. Check the evidence. Inspect the matching terms and stored research details, then open the original university source.
  3. Compare your shortlist. Save researchers and compare up to three by research focus, location, and profile fit.
  4. Prepare your message. Write and review one editable outreach draft. Copy it into your email app when you are ready.

Résumé import suggests skills, courses and experience for you to review before adding them to your profile.
